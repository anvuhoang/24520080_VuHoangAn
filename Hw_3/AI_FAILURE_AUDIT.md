## 5. Lessons Learned & AI Collaboration Protocol
1. **Never accept time calculation code without explicit UTC and drift checks:** AIs frequently output naive `new Date()` differences that fail on timezone offsets and device clock drift.
2. **Always demand progressive enhancement first:** AI agents default to single-page, script-reliant paradigms unless explicitly constrained to build accessible HTML fallbacks.
3. **Validate form states defensively:** AIs often omit loading indicators and double-click safeguards, opening vulnerability to race conditions.
